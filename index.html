Field guide

Solve. Sail. Collect.

1. Unlock a port

Choose a focused skill or a mixed expedition covering all four: distributive property, combining like terms, multi-step equations, and variables on both sides. Work on scratch paper before entering your answer.

For expressions, enter the coefficient of x and the constant term in the form ax + b. For example, −2(x + 3) becomes −2x − 6: enter −2 for the coefficient and −6 for the constant. Enter 0 for a missing term. For equations, enter the value of x. Decimals and fractions such as 3/2 are accepted.

2. Learn as you go

Check your solution for feedback. Hints and retries are unlimited. After two incorrect attempts, you may study the worked solution. You still need to enter it correctly to unlock your voyage. First-try results record whether your first submitted answer was correct; hints and solution views are counted separately.

3. Sail for treasure

Each correct solution earns a 14-move turn. Collect up to three gems, worth 25 points each, and reach the gold exit for 50 bonus points. Use ↑ ↓ ← → or W A S D while the board is focused. You can also use the arrow buttons or tap a neighboring tile. Rocks and edges block movement without using a move. Dock early whenever you like.

The activity is untimed. Arcade points measure treasure collected; the summary reports your math separately. Sound is optional and off by default.

Wrap up this expedition?

Your summary will include the problems you have solved so far. An unfinished problem or treasure run will not count as a cleared port. Treasure already collected stays in your score.
